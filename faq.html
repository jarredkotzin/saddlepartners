Questions that are asked frequently

We're a team of marketing nerds and data junkies who help brands win. We'll manage your campaigns, own your SEO, and build the data systems that power your growth engine. We mainly do Google Ads today.

We do a lot of things, but broadly, they fit into three buckets.

(1) Data infrastructure: making sure data is being captured, cleaned, and stored effectively so that it can be used to power campaigns.

(2) Campaign management: everything from account structure, bid strategy, targeting, and budget allocation all driven by data.

(3) Reporting: providing your team visibility into the metrics that actually matter (e.g. CAC, LTV, qualified pipeline, etc.)

The short answer is: it depends. On a lot of things.

But that probably isn't very helpful. So here's the long answer.

Most performance marketing agencies structure their fee as a percentage of monthly ad spend. We think this can create misaligned incentives.

If you ask your hairdresser if they think you need a haircut, they'll probably say yes.

If you ask a performance ad agency who gets paid a percentage of your ad spend if you should spend more money on advertising, they'll probably say yes.

We'll do a full audit of your existing account, share a summary of where we think we can provide a lot of value, and offer a proposed price for those services.

It will probably be around 15-20% of monthly ad spend, but the final number will ultimately depend on a variety of factors.

And it won't be carried forward as a percentage of monthly ad spend. It will be a flat fee. That is, unless scope changes meaningfully.
